# Annual Recommendation on Infrastructure, Staffing, and Technical Priorities

- **Issue:** #17 (stays open)
- **Driver:** Luke
- **Cadence:** annual

## How it runs

Management note (LOE sheet, 2026-09-22): the recommendation is delivered in the form of
requirements - a list of what is needed (## CPUs, ## GB of RAM, ## GPUs) - and a quote,
assembled with guidance from Ed Hazeldine or Michael Perkins, who have been asked to help.

## Records

One file per year, `YYYY.md`, closing that year's recurrence sub-issue: the requirements
list, the quote, and who it went to.
